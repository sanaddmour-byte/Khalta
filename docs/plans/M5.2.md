# M5.2 — Strength intelligence

Status: **draft, awaiting approval.** No application code is written until this plan is approved.

Feature in scope (`06-features.md`): **F-028** Strength models and low-strength alerts. Spec refs: `01-domain.md` §3 (strength w/cm, model validity), §4 (model invalidation), §8 (new strength results → refit, update s; cement-reduction opportunity or risk alert), `02-codes.md` §6 (β_FM, β_75 null until calibrated), `03-ui.md` §N (low-strength alerts on the Dashboard); `04-phases.md` M5.2 ("Model fitting with validity and held-out checks, s refit, low-strength alerts, β calibration proposal. Done: alert fires on the seeded low-strength sequence; model invalidation tests pass").

## 1. Business objective

Let the plant's own strength results speak. When enough results exist under one cement source, SCM, admixture family and specimen basis, Khalta fits a plant strength curve, says exactly how far it can be trusted, and — only after a QC manager approves it — lets the optimizer use it instead of the published ACI baseline. It watches new results against the acceptance rules and raises a risk alert early, or notes steady overstrength as a **theoretical** cement-reduction opportunity that still needs a trial. Nothing here changes a design, loosens a limit or approves anything by itself.

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

1. **Fitting (pure, in `packages/engine/src/strength/`).** Per group (plant, cement source, SCM family, admixture family, specimen basis, test age): ordinary least squares of ln f = a − b·(w/cm), with the standard error of a and b, the residual standard deviation s (in MPa, from results around the curve), and **held-out diagnostics** (leave-one-out, or k-fold when n is large): mean error, RMSE, worst miss, and the share of held-out results above the prediction minus 1.64·s. All arithmetic in decimals where it feeds a stored figure; the fit is deterministic and tested against hand-worked data.
2. **Validity (§3, exactly as written).** `valid` needs ≥ 30 results at the test age, ≥ 3 distinct w/cm levels spanning ≥ 0.10, one group key, results within the last 12 months, b > 0 and held-out checks passing the tenant limits; fewer results or levels, or a failed held-out check, gives `provisional` with the **named** reason; a change of group (new cement source, SCM, admixture family, specimen basis) or results outside the domain gives `invalidated`. A model never silently becomes valid: statuses are recomputed and every change is logged.
3. **Where w/cm and the group come from.** Each strength result already belongs to a design version; at fit time the model reads that version's stored evaluation (effective w/cm) and its material lines (cement source, SCM, admixture family, specimen basis). Results whose design has no usable evaluation, or whose materials are not identifiable, are **excluded and listed with the reason**; nothing is guessed.
4. **Approval and use.** A fitted model is a **proposal**. A QC manager approves it with a reason and e-signature (four-eyes against whoever triggered the fit is not needed; the approver is a QC manager). Only an **approved, valid** model is used: the evaluator and the optimizer then compute the strength-governed w/cm from the model for targets inside its domain (evidence `MODEL_IN_DOMAIN` + `TRIAL_REQUIRED`), and fall back to the ACI 211.1 baseline otherwise (`MODEL_BASELINE`). The durability and code ceilings still win: final ceiling = min(strength w/cm, durability max w/cm) − robustness margin. The independent validator recomputes the model's predicted strength from the stored coefficients with its own arithmetic.
5. **s refit.** From the same results the standard deviation of the plant's production is re-estimated and shown with n and the ACI 318 / JS minimum-sample conditions. It is a **proposal** to change the f′cr margin: shown, never applied automatically; applying it is a QC-manager decision on the Rules screen (existing verify flow). A lower s may only be **proposed** when its conditions are met; a higher s is flagged immediately.
6. **Low-strength alerts (extends the M5.1 per-set alert).** On every new strength result for an approved or in-production design: the **running average of three consecutive tests** against f′c and the individual-test criterion (per the acceptance rules in the rules package, never hard-coded in the alert), plus a **sequence rule**: N consecutive sets below the model's lower prediction band. A breach is a **critical** insight (banner, inbox) naming the design, the tests and the criterion that failed; the QC manager may then suspend (existing manual flow). Same dedupe, snooze and dismiss as M5.1; an alert expires when a later set restores the criterion.
7. **Cement-reduction opportunity.** When a valid approved model exists and a design's results are consistently above f′cr by more than a tenant margin, the existing M5.1 opportunity path re-runs the optimizer with the model; if it finds a validator-passed, cheaper, compliant candidate at the same snapshot it is a **theoretical** opportunity (trial-only draft on Accept). No new path: the optimizer simply sees the model.
8. **β calibration proposal.** From logged trial batches with water, slump and the aggregates' FM and 75 µm values, a least-squares **proposal** for β_FM and β_75 (with n, standard errors and the batches used) when enough batches exist and the design of experiments allows it (varying FM and P75); otherwise "not enough data" with what is missing. A QC manager enters a value on the Rules screen if they accept it; the code never writes `eng.water.beta_*` itself.
9. **Screens.** A **Strength** section in the Library design sheet (results, the curve with the data and prediction band, running average of 3) already exists from M4.2; M5.2 adds a **Strength models** page (list per group with status chip, n, w/cm span, s, held-out diagnostics, excluded results with reasons, approve / retire), the s-refit and β proposals panels, low-strength alerts in the Insights inbox (type exists) and the Dashboard tile data endpoint (the Dashboard screen itself is later).
10. **Jobs.** Triggered by the M5.1 strength-result job (refit the affected group, update status, alerts) and the nightly sweep (re-check validity: age of results, changed groups). Debounced per group; idempotent.
11. **API.** `GET /api/strength-models`, `GET /api/strength-models/:id`, `POST /api/strength-models/refit` (QC roles, per plant), `POST /api/strength-models/:id/approve` and `/retire` (QC manager, e-signature), `GET /api/strength/s-proposal`, `GET /api/strength/beta-proposal`. Audit on every mutation; cost never involved.
12. **i18n, tests, docs.** EN/AR strings (Arabic drafts in `docs/i18n-review.md`); ADR 0014 (strength model validity, held-out method, approval, β proposal); engine tests with hand-worked fits, property tests (fit invariant to row order and to uniform w/cm shift conventions, status never `valid` below any threshold, prediction monotone decreasing in w/cm, held-out never uses the held row); **validity matrix tests** (each threshold on both sides); **invalidation tests** (new cement source, SCM, admixture family, basis, stale results, out-of-domain target); API tests with real Postgres (fit from the seeded SYNTHETIC set, approve, optimizer uses the model only when approved and valid and in domain, RBAC and plant scope, e-signature recorded); alert test on the **seeded low-strength sequence**; e2e with axe; screens EN/AR × 1440 / 1024 / 390.

**Explicitly excluded**

- Any automatic approval, automatic change of f′cr or of a rule value, automatic suspension; non-linear or multi-variable strength models; early-age or maturity prediction; the Dashboard page itself; cement-source change management beyond invalidation; real plant results (SYNTHETIC only in tests and e2e).

## 3. Assumptions

- Test age for a model is the design's `test_age_days` (default 28); one model per age.
- The acceptance rules for running averages are read from the rules package (ACI 318 / JS rows already seeded, unverified); an unverified or missing rule makes the alert **provisional and named**, not silent.
- Specimen basis is cylinder or cube per result; results of different bases never mix.
- Tenant limits for held-out checks and the overstrength margin are settings with documented defaults.

## 5. Engineering-correctness risks

| Risk                                                     | Mitigation                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A model fitted on mixed cements or admixtures is trusted | Group key enforced; any change invalidates; excluded results listed; tests for each key part                                    |
| Overfitting or extrapolation gives an unsafe w/cm        | Domain check (w/cm span and age), held-out limits, durability ceiling always wins, `TRIAL_REQUIRED` stays, validator recomputes |
| A model used before a human sees it                      | Only approved + valid + in-domain models are used; approval is e-signed; retire at any time                                     |
| s refit loosens the f′cr margin automatically            | Proposal only; a lower s needs the code's sample conditions and a QC manager's Rules edit                                       |
| Alerts too noisy or silent                               | Dedupe, expiry, thresholds from rules; the seeded sequence fires exactly once; a recovering sequence expires it                 |
| Stale data treated as current                            | 12-month window, nightly revalidation, status change logged and shown with the reason                                           |
| β proposed from confounded data                          | Proposal needs variation in FM and P75 and a minimum n; standard errors shown; never written by code                            |

## 6. Schema / API / UI impact

- **Schema (0024):** `strength_models` (as in `01-domain` §Data model: group key parts, coefficients a and b with standard errors, n, distinct w/cm levels, w/cm and age domain, s, held-out diagnostics jsonb, status, fitted_at, approved_by, retired_at), `strength_model_points` (result id, w/cm used, included or the exclusion reason; append-only per fit), `design_transitions`-style e-signature reference for approval; settings columns for held-out limits and overstrength margin. Additive only.
- **Engine:** `strength/` (fit, validity, held-out, s refit, β proposal), optimizer and evaluator accept an optional approved model; the validator gets a small independent recomputation (types only from the engine).
- **API/worker:** routes above; strength-result job extended; nightly revalidation.
- **UI:** Strength models page, proposals panels, approve / retire dialogs (e-signature), alert wording in the inbox.

## 7. Verification strategy

- Definition of Done commands; engine and validator coverage ≥ 90 %.
- **Phase gates as tests:** the **seeded low-strength sequence** fires the alert exactly once and expires when it recovers; the **model invalidation** matrix passes (each key part and each threshold).
- Hand-worked fit fixtures (the OLS, s and held-out numbers reproduced independently), property tests as in item 12, optimizer golden: with no approved model the output is byte-identical to M5.1 (no behavior change by default).
- **Verification note** (in `docs/progress.md`): tests prove the fit arithmetic, the validity and invalidation rules, that only approved valid in-domain models are used, and the alert triggers as implemented. They do **not** prove that the fitted curve describes your plants, that your results are clean, or that the SYNTHETIC set resembles yours.

## 8. Rollback / migration

Additive tables and columns; with no approved model the optimizer and evaluator behave as before. Rollback = revert the commit; no existing record changes meaning.

## 9. Questions for you (default in brackets)

1. **Model use:** only after QC-manager approval with e-signature [yes]; or use any `valid` model automatically [no].
2. **Held-out limits for `valid`:** held-out RMSE ≤ 1.5 × s and no held-out miss beyond 3 s [defaults, tenant setting].
3. **Overstrength margin for a cement-reduction opportunity:** results' average at least 10 % above f′cr, over ≥ 10 sets [defaults, tenant setting].
4. **Sequence alert:** 3 consecutive sets below the lower prediction band (mean − 1.64 s) [default].
5. **s refit:** proposal only, applied by hand on the Rules screen [yes].
6. **β calibration:** proposal only, minimum 12 trial batches with varied FM and 75 µm [default].
7. **Seeded sequence:** SYNTHETIC, in tests and e2e only [yes].
