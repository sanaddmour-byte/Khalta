# M3.1 — Controlled optimizer

Status: **approved with defaults (2026-10-02); built.** All ten questions below were answered "use your defaults". Deviations from this plan are listed in `docs/progress.md`.

Features in scope (`06-features.md`): **F-017** Optimizer (lowest delivered material cost per plant), **F-014** Mix characteristics Auto / Fixed / Range / Target, **F-015** Degrees-of-freedom meter and conflict diagnostics, **F-016** Objective modes. Spec refs: `01-domain.md` §4 (optimizer), §5 (rounding), §3.1 (validator), §7 (tests); `07-characteristics.md` §1, §4; `02-codes.md` §5. The Design Studio, characteristics panel, DoF meter and conflict panel are **M3.2**; here they exist as engine, API and tests (F-014/F-015/F-016 acceptance criteria are proven at that level).

## 1. Business objective

Generate **trial-only** candidate mixes at the lowest delivered material cost for a plant, from the plant's own materials, tests and prices, that satisfy every resolved hard requirement and every characteristic the engineer set, and prove each one with the independent validator after rounding. A candidate is a proposal that needs a trial and QC approval; the optimizer never produces anything that can reach `approved`.

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

1. **Solver and formulation (ADR 0009).** HiGHS (`highs`, MIT, WASM) behind a small interface. The formulation of `01-domain.md` §4.2 per configuration (variables B, W, Vᵢ; rows C1–C9) with a **written linearity argument for every term** (`docs/formulation-m3.1.md`) and regression fixtures. Solved in a Node worker thread so requests never block the API.
2. **Enumeration (§4.1).** Cement product × SCM product × SCM fraction p (steps from settings, up to the governing maximum) × admixture × dosage level (3–5 from its table) × NMAS when free. Cement properties are checked by C₃A (never by label) against the cross-code equivalence; pruning by lower-bound cost; cap 200 configurations, coarse-to-fine above that; non-converged fixed-point configurations are dropped and logged.
3. **Strength and water (no plant model yet).** w/cm ceiling = min(ACI 211.1 w/c for f′cr, durability maximum) − robustness margin (`eng.margin.wcm`); water from the ACI 211.1 table (interpolated) reduced by the admixture's dosage table, **never** presented as a plant prediction. Every candidate carries `MODEL_BASELINE` + `TRIAL_REQUIRED`. The deterministic **ACI 211.1 baseline proportioning path** (§4.4) gives the starting point and the golden test.
4. **Guardrails and grading** exactly as §4.2 (C5 envelope rows by mass, C6 coarseness, C7 workability with the fixed-point loop, C8 fines/pumpability, C9 availability). **If a required value is null the request is blocked and the missing parameters are named** (see §4, §10): nothing is guessed.
5. **Characteristic rows (07 §4).** Fixed → equality, range → inequalities, target → deviation variables, all `USER_SPECIFIED`; `USER_OVERRIDE` where a characteristic replaces a model estimate or guardrail (QC role, reason recorded); "slump at risk" when fixed water is below the model estimate by more than the settings threshold; `MODEL_PREDICTS_SHORTFALL` when the user's w/cm is above the strength-baseline w/cm (cannot become `trial_candidate` without QC authorization). Degrees of freedom (free quantities minus equalities): positive / zero ("fully specified: evaluate only") / negative.
6. **Conflict diagnostics (07 §4.2).** Re-solve with elastic slacks on `USER_SPECIFIED` rows only (hard rows stay hard); report each conflicting characteristic with the value that would work; if infeasible with every user row released, report the binding hard rows as diagnostics with **no saving attached**.
7. **Objective modes (07 §4.3).** `cheapest` (default) and `closest_to_targets` (two-stage lexicographic: minimum weighted deviation, then lowest cost within a tolerance); `max_cost_jod_m3` applies in both.
8. **Rounding and finalization (§5).** Cement/SCM up to 5 kg, water 1 kg, admixture 0.01 L, aggregates 5 kg; rebalance volume to 1.000 m³ on the largest fine aggregate; re-validate from scratch; up to 3 cement top-ups; reject if rebalancing breaks grading or a guardrail; every characteristic re-checked within its rounding tolerance; cost recomputed on the rounded quantities.
9. **Independent validator (`validateCandidate`).** In `packages/validator`, with its own arithmetic: recomputes volumes, w/cm, binder/SCM, grading, chlorides, availability, guardrails, every resolved hard constraint and every characteristic on the rounded candidate; it imports no optimizer code (lint + dependency-cruiser stay). Validator failure overrides solver status.
10. **Persistence and API.** `design_requests` (inputs snapshot: requirements, ruleset mode, characteristics, objective, price basis, material versions, rule versions) and `design_candidates` (rank, cost, binder, w/cm, governing constraints, binding rows, guardrail shadow prices, proportions, evidence, validator result, solver metadata), immutable. `POST /api/design-requests` (generate), `GET /api/design-requests/:id[/candidates]`, `POST /api/design-requests/:id/candidates/:cid/trial-candidate` (creates a **`trial_candidate`** design only after the validator passed after rounding; lifecycle edge `draft → trial_candidate` turned on; `MODEL_PREDICTS_SHORTFALL` needs QC-manager authorization). Cost fields stripped without `cost.view`; evaluation of a candidate is the same stored evaluation as M2.1.
11. **Top-N output (§4.3):** default 5 ranked by cost with evidence, margins on governing constraints, binding hard rows as diagnostics only, guardrail **shadow prices** (HiGHS duals, guardrail and preference rows only) labelled hypothetical, QC roles only.
12. **Tests and ADR**: ACI 211.1 golden test (see §10 Q2), adversarial tests, every characteristic × every supported mode satisfied exactly (property tests) and re-checked by the validator, conflict fixtures naming the right characteristics, DoF cases (0, positive, negative), `closest_to_targets` minimum-deviation then cheapest, "no hard-rule relaxation output anywhere", every candidate trial-only, performance budget (typical request < 5 s; cap 200 configurations).

**Explicitly excluded**

- Studio UI, characteristics panel, DoF meter, conflict panel, quick-entry drawer, candidate comparison (**M3.2**); profiles (**M3.3**); trial batches, approval, PDFs (M4.x); strength models and results (M4.2/M5.2); plant-calibrated water model (β terms stay null until ≥ 5 trial batches); air-entrained designs (tables not on file: such requests are blocked with a clear message); haul, pumping, carbon and rejection cost terms (ADR required).
- Any output that relaxes a CODE_HARD / PROJECT_HARD row, or attaches a saving to an infeasible result.

## 3. Assumptions

- All 79 not-on-file rule values and every unverified ACI value stay as they are; candidates are provisional and labelled `RULE_UNVERIFIED` while rules are unverified.
- A candidate's strength is **not** predicted; it is `MODEL_BASELINE` + `TRIAL_REQUIRED` by construction. f′cr above 40 MPa or below 15 MPa is outside the ACI 211.1 table: blocked, not extrapolated.
- Aggregate prices come from the plant's live prices (preferred supplier) at the request date or a named snapshot; unpriced or ambiguous materials are unavailable (C9), never zero-cost.
- The HiGHS WASM package installs from the registry (checked: `highs` 1.15.3, MIT). If it ever cannot be used, the fallback is `javascript-lp-solver` through an ADR, as the spec says.

## 5. Engineering-correctness risks

| Risk                                                | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Optimizer and validator share a bug                 | Validator is a separate implementation (exact rationals); corruption tests alter solver output, rounding and traces and are detected without reading solver flags |
| A hard row is silently relaxed to find an answer    | Hard rows are never elastic; only `USER_SPECIFIED` rows get slack; property test: no output exists with a hard-row violation; diagnostics carry no saving         |
| Rounding breaks a limit                             | Round, rebalance, re-validate from scratch; top-up loop; reject on failure; property test over generated requests                                                 |
| Missing parameter treated as "no limit"             | Missing grading/WF/fines/pumpability values block the request and are named; test per parameter                                                                   |
| Nonlinear term forced into an LP                    | Written linearity argument per term; fixed-point loop for the non-homogeneous ones, 0.1 % / 5 iterations; non-converged configurations dropped and logged         |
| Candidate mistaken for an approved or predicted mix | `TRIAL_REQUIRED` + `MODEL_BASELINE` on every candidate; no code path from candidate to `approved`; lifecycle test; copy states "trial required"                   |
| Cost leak, savings claims                           | Cost stripped for roles without `cost.view`; candidates carry cost only; no savings figure until M3.2/M5.1 and then only theoretical                              |
| HiGHS WASM memory/time in the API process           | Worker thread, per-request time and configuration caps, cancellation; load test; fallback ADR path                                                                |
| Solution differs by platform/run                    | Deterministic enumeration order, fixed tolerances, results rounded; same request → byte-identical candidates (test)                                               |

## 6. Schema / API / UI impact

- **Schema (migrations 0014+):** `design_requests`, `design_candidates` (immutable, append-only triggers, no hard delete); tenant settings keys (`scmSteps`, `dosageLevels`, `waterOverrideWarnPct`, `maxConfigurations`, `candidatesTopN`).
- **API:** the endpoints in scope item 10; OpenAPI entries; RBAC `design.write` to generate, `trial.request` to create a trial candidate (QC roles), `cost.view` for costs, QC manager to authorize `MODEL_PREDICTS_SHORTFALL`.
- **Engine:** `optimizer/` (formulation, enumeration, baseline path, solve, conflicts, rounding, finalization), pure with the solver injected; **validator:** `validateCandidate`.
- **UI:** none beyond the generated OpenAPI (Studio is M3.2). Existing Rules screen lets you enter the missing parameters; the evaluation tab already shows candidates once saved as designs.

## 7. Verification strategy

- Definition of Done commands; `pnpm test` coverage ≥ 90 % on engine and validator; dependency rule keeps the validator independent of the optimizer.
- **Golden test:** ACI 211.1 worked example through the baseline path (cement ± 3, water ± 3, aggregates ± 10 kg/m³, volume ± 0.002 m³).
- **Property tests (fast-check):** every candidate sums to 1.000 m³; cost is monotone in every price; tightening w/cm never decreases binder; rounding never breaks a hard limit; Both mode never less strict than either code; every characteristic × mode satisfied exactly and validated; determinism.
- **Adversarial tests:** infeasible hard rows → diagnostics only, no empty result, no relaxation with a saving; missing parameters blocked and named; contradictory characteristics; zero available aggregates; ambiguous prices; f′cr out of table; air-entrained blocked; non-converging fixed point.
- **Validator corruption suite** for candidates; conflict fixtures; DoF cases; `closest_to_targets` two-stage; performance (200 configurations within budget).
- **API tests (real Postgres):** generate, blockers, immutability, trial-candidate creation rules and lifecycle, RBAC, plant scope, cost stripping, audit.
- **Verification note** (in `docs/progress.md`): tests prove the formulation is solved exactly, constraints hold after rounding, and the validator agrees. They do **not** prove a candidate will reach strength or slump in your plant (baseline estimates, trial required), that any rule value is right, or that the synthetic parameters resemble yours.

## 8. Rollback / migration

Additive: two tables, settings keys, new modules and one dependency (`highs`). Rollback = revert commits and drop the tables on dev databases; candidates are derived data and can be regenerated from the stored request snapshot.
